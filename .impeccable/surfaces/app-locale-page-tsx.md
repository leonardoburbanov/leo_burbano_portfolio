# Home (portfolio) surface brief

Scope: home page `app/[locale]/page.tsx` and the shared tokens it sets for `/events` and `/blog`. Mode: Experience. Audience: mixed (hiring managers, clients, community), arriving cold from LinkedIn. Action: Connect on LinkedIn.

User-pinned direction (2026-10-23): minimalist, high-impact, in the register of Apple / NVIDIA product pages; no "boxes" (card grids); palette black, white, grays with nuances of a blue gradient (royal blue to sky cyan, from the user's "IA & Robótica" reference). This pin overrides the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A product launch page where the product is a production engineer: proof set at keynote scale on a dark stage. It refuses the category default of rounded cards, chips and badge rows.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black stage (#050608) with warm-white type (#f5f5f7) and Apple-gray secondary (#86868b); light theme mirrors it (#fbfbfd / #1d1d1f / #6e6e73). One blue gradient (#3d6ef5 → #4f9cf0 → #5ccbe6) used for a single headline phrase per screen, the primary pill and the top-of-page light. No cards, no chips: content sits on the ground, separated by space and 1px hairlines. Geist Sans, 600 display weight, tight tracking.

STORY: The visitor learns who he is and what he ships in one viewport, sees the scale proven as a spec sheet, reads the system, the career and the work as clean rows, then connects on LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered. Name at ~6rem, under it "Senior AI Engineer & Tech Lead" in the blue gradient at ~2.5rem, value proposition in gray (max 40ch), then one tracked line of the stack with logos separated by blue dots, company marks as monochrome logos, blue pill "Connect on LinkedIn" plus a chevron text link to the case study. A soft blue light falls from above the fold.

FORM: User-pinned canon (Apple/NVIDIA keynote page), not from the roll list; seed:86f4961f77ff7038.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
